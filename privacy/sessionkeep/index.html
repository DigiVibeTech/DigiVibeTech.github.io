SessionKeep privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026. Publisher: DigiVibeTech (Netherlands). Contact: digivibetech.com/support

SessionKeep stores the titles and addresses of your tabs so you can restore them later. That's all it does with them.

  • Everything is kept in your browser's local extension storage on your own computer. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. There is no account and no server.
  • Private / incognito windows are never saved.
  • If you turn on backup files, SessionKeep writes them to your Downloads folder. Where they go after that is up to you.
  • No analytics, no ads, no remote code.
  • Removing the extension deletes its storage. Back up first if you want to keep your sessions.
