Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Markdown & llms.txt Export for Confluence (“the App”) is provided by DigiVibeTech, an eenmanszaak registered in the Netherlands (KvK 98384848), digivibetech.com/support.

Where the App runs

The App is built on Atlassian Forge and runs entirely on Atlassian's infrastructure. DigiVibeTech operates no servers of its own for this App.

What data the App processes

When you start an export, the App reads the selected pages, their metadata (title, id, version, URL) and attachment names through Confluence's APIs, converts them to Markdown and returns the result to your browser. Processing happens within your request.

What data the App stores

None. The App does not store page content, exports or personal data, and sends nothing to DigiVibeTech or any third party. No analytics or tracking.

Personal data

Page content may contain personal data entered by your users. The App only transforms it on your instruction and does not keep it. Atlassian processes data under its own privacy policy and the Atlassian Marketplace terms.

Billing

Licences are sold and invoiced by Atlassian. DigiVibeTech receives licence information (site and licence tier) from Atlassian, not payment details.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us about any data we hold about you; we reply within 30 days. You can complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (the Dutch data protection authority).

Changes

We will publish changes on this page and update the date above.
