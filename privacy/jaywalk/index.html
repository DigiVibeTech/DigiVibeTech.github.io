Jaywalk privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026. Publisher: DigiVibeTech (Netherlands).

Jaywalk does not collect, store, send or sell any data.

  • It makes no network requests of its own. The JSON you view is already loaded by your browser; Jaywalk only reformats it on your screen.
  • Text you paste into the format/compare page stays on that page and is gone when you close it.
  • The only things it saves are your theme choice and any sites you told it to skip, in the browser's local extension storage.
  • No analytics, no ads, no affiliate links, no remote code.

Why it asks to "read and change data on all websites": the extension has to look at a page to know whether it is a JSON response. On any page that is not JSON it stops straight away and changes nothing.
