FAQ

How do I get help?

E-mail contact@digivibetech.com with the product name, your browser or Jira/Confluence site, and what you expected to happen. We aim to reply within 2 business days (CET).

Do your extensions send my data anywhere?

No. TidyMD, Jaywalk and SessionKeep work entirely in your browser, with no analytics or tracking. See the privacy policies.

How do I pay for or restore TidyMD Pro?

Pro is handled by ExtensionPay with Stripe. Click "Upgrade" or log in from the TidyMD popup with the email you paid with; your purchase works on any browser. To cancel, use the same login to manage your subscription.

TidyMD Pro did not unlock in Brave.

Set Brave Shields to "down" for extensionpay.com (or allow its cookies), then log in again from the TidyMD popup.

A TidyMD AI chat export is missing older messages.

Chat sites load long chats lazily. Scroll to the top of the chat, then export again.

Jaywalk does not format a page.

Jaywalk only formats real JSON responses. Check the site is not on your skip list in the Jaywalk options.

I lost sessions in SessionKeep.

Sessions are stored locally; removing the extension deletes them. Turn on backup files in settings to keep copies in your Downloads folder.

Who sells and bills the Jira and Confluence apps?

Atlassian Marketplace. Licences, invoices and refunds go through Atlassian; contact us for product questions.
